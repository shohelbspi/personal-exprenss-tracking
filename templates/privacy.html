Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the service, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Spendly service — for example, to display your expenses, generate spending summaries, and keep your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected using reasonable technical and administrative safeguards. While we work to keep your information secure, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting and email delivery. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose.

We do not share your personal or financial data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
